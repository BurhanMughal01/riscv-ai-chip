## Potential Agreements

Subject to verification of ownership and third-party rights, possible arrangements include:

1. Exclusive commercial license
2. Non-exclusive commercial license
3. Technology transfer
4. Custom development agreement
5. Silicon implementation partnership

## Rights Verification

Before entering an agreement, the following must be assessed:

- Human-authored contributions
- AI-generated code
- Third-party dependencies
- Existing public disclosures
- Copyright and patent eligibility
- Any prior licenses or permissions

## No Warranty

No guarantee of silicon readiness, patent protection, freedom from infringement, or exclusive copyright ownership is made by this document.

## Contractual Requirements

Any commercial agreement must be written, reviewed by a qualified lawyer and signed by legally authorized parties.
